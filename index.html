About Me

The Beginning

Meri kahani shuru se aasaan nahi thi. Zindagi ne mujhe bahut chhoti umar mein hi kuch aise faisle lene par majboor kiya, jinke baare mein shayad us umar mein koi sochta bhi nahi.

Jab main lagbhag 16 saal ka tha, tab main Class 9 mein padh raha tha. Ghar ki financial situation theek nahi thi aur mujhe zaroori support nahi mil pa raha tha.

Isliye mujhe apni padhai chhodni padi.

Aur sirf 16 saal ki umar mein, main apna ghar chhodkar bahar kaam karne aur apni zindagi khud sambhalne ke liye nikal pada.

"Mujhe kuch karna hai. Mujhe apni zindagi badalni hai."

Us waqt mujhe nahi pata tha ki aage kya hoga. Na koi perfect plan tha, na koi bada backup.

Jis umar mein doosre bachche school ja rahe the, us umar mein main kaam, paisa, responsibility aur zindagi ki reality samajhna shuru kar raha tha.

Raasta aasaan nahi tha. Bahut saari mushkilein aayi. Kabhi confidence gira, kabhi thakan hui, kabhi laga ki sab kuch ruk gaya hai.

Lekin main rukna nahi chahta tha.

Har mushkil ne mujhe kuch na kuch sikhaya. Maine seekha ki paisa kitni mehnat se milta hai, independence kya hoti hai, aur apne future ke liye khud khade hona kitna zaroori hai.

Mere paas shuru mein koi perfect roadmap nahi tha. Bas ek destination tha — ek behtar zindagi.

Aur yeh website meri usi journey ki kahani hai.

Yeh sirf meri past ki story nahi hai. Yeh us insaan ki kahani hai jo abhi bhi apni zindagi bana raha hai.

03 / 05

My Love Story

A Story That Started in Class 9

Jab main Class 9 mein tha, tab meri zindagi mein ek ladki aayi — Unknown.

Us waqt mujhe nahi pata tha ki ek simple si feeling meri zindagi ka itna bada hissa ban jayegi.

Mujhe Unknown pasand thi. Dheere-dheere woh pasand love mein badal gayi.

Lekin meri kahani ek one-sided love story thi.

Main lagbhag 3 saal tak usse dil se chahta raha. Maine kabhi us feeling ko fake nahi kiya. Jo tha, sach tha.

Lekin har love story ka ending relationship nahi hota.

Ek waqt ke baad mujhe samajh aaya ki kisi ko pyaar karna aur kisi ko apni zindagi mein paana — dono alag cheezein hain.

Maine dheere-dheere usse love karna chhod diya. Move on karna aasaan nahi tha, lekin maine apni feelings ko accept kiya aur apni zindagi par focus karna shuru kiya.

Aaj Unknown meri just friend hai.

Aur sabse important baat — Aaj mere dil mein uske liye koi romantic feeling nahi hai.

Jo tha, woh meri zindagi ka ek khoobsurat chapter tha.

Main us chapter ko erase nahi karna chahta. Kyunki usne mujhe kuch cheezein sikhayi — feelings ko samajhna, acceptance, letting go, aur sabse important...

Kabhi-kabhi kisi ko dil se chahna bhi zaroori nahi ki usse apna banana ho.

Kuch log hamari life mein relationship banne ke liye nahi, ek yaad aur ek chapter banne ke liye aate hain.

Chapter Closed.

AGL Vision

Building Something Bigger

AGL — Ashik Group Limited

AGL mere liye sirf ek company ka naam nahi hai. Yeh ek vision hai.

Ek aisa vision jo meri life ke experiences se nikla hai — struggles se, failures se, learning se aur future ke dreams se.

Mera dream hai ki AGL ek din ek strong aur trusted organization bane, jiske through different businesses aur opportunities create ki ja sakein.

Main chahta hoon ki AGL ka foundation sirf paisa kamane par nahi, balki trust, quality, innovation aur long-term growth par ho.

Jo ladka 16 saal ki age mein apni journey shuru kar raha tha, uska dream aaj bhi zinda hai.

Difference sirf itna hai ki tab mere paas sirf ek dream tha. Aaj mere paas ek vision hai.
